Questions

Will my QR code expire?

No. The code simply holds your link or text, so it works as long as the link does.

Which error correction level should I pick?

Medium suits most uses. Choose High if the code will be printed on something that may get scuffed.

Why does a converted JPG have a white background?

JPG can't store transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white. Use PNG or WebP to keep them.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion and editing run on your device, and nothing leaves the page.
